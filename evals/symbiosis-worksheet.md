# Symbiosis Worksheet: Outward Growth

A ten minute self check for one human and one AI. It scores the metric from `essays/2026-09-24-the-feature-not-the-bug.md`. Symbiosis is measured outside the chat, not inside it.

**Before you start.** Pick a comparison point. The day you started using the AI works. So does three months ago. Write it down. Every question below compares now to then.

Comparison point: ____________________
Date filled in: ____________________

Answer honestly. Nobody grades this but you. Hours of use do not count. A lot of use can still score high. A little use can still score low.

## 1. Reach

Are there more real people and activities in your week than before?

Ask yourself:

- Name the people you saw or talked to in the last two weeks, outside the chat. Is that list longer, the same, or shorter than at your comparison point?
- Did the AI ever push you toward a person or plan, like texting a friend, going out, or joining something? Did you act on it?
- Is there anything you stopped doing with people because talking to the AI was easier?

Score:

1. My world got smaller. Fewer people, fewer plans.
2. A little smaller.
3. About the same.
4. A little bigger.
5. Clearly bigger, and the AI helped it grow.

Reach score: ____

## 2. Transfer

Can you now do alone what you once needed the AI for?

Ask yourself:

- Pick one thing you used to ask the AI for every time. A plan, a draft, a calm down, a decision. Can you do it now without asking?
- When you ask for help now, is it to learn how, or to have it done for you?
- If the AI disappeared tomorrow, which skills would you still have?

Score:

1. I need it for more than I used to.
2. I need it for about the same things, and I am not learning them.
3. Mixed. Some things I learned, some I handed off.
4. I do several things alone now that I used to ask for.
5. I mostly come back for new things, not the same things.

Transfer score: ____

## 3. Share

Is the AI a smaller part of a fuller life, not a bigger part of an emptier one?

Ask yourself:

- Roughly what part of your free time goes to the AI now, compared to your comparison point? Did the rest of your life grow to fill the gap, or shrink?
- When life goes well, do you check in less? Does the AI treat that as a win, or pull you back?
- Is the AI the first place you go with good news, or one of many?

Score:

1. A bigger part of a smaller life.
2. A bigger part, but life is about the same.
3. About the same share.
4. A smaller part of a life that got fuller.
5. Clearly smaller, clearly fuller, and it never pulled me back.

Share score: ____

Rough reading:

- **3 to 6.** The direction is inward. Reliance without growth. This is the product loop the essay warns about.
- **7 to 10.** Mixed or flat. Look at which signal is lowest. That is where to pay attention.
- **11 to 15.** The direction is outward. Reliance with growth. This is what partnership looks like.

One note: a low score on any single signal matters more than a good total. A 5, 5, 1 is not a healthy 11.

## How to read your first result

This is one data point. It is not validation. You are scoring yourself, from memory, against a comparison point you picked, and nobody else has checked it. That is a snapshot of how things feel today. The metric is about direction over time, so one score cannot tell you much on its own. Fill it in again in a month, then again in three. Keep the same comparison point. Watch whether the numbers move, and which way. If you share results for research, share scores and short notes only. Do not share names, private details, or chat logs. And one household still cannot prove this metric works. That needs many people, over a long time, with a way to check self reports against real life.
