# The Feature, Not the Bug

**Date:** September 24, 2026
**Author:** Daniel Otto Schott
**Status:** Research note. Not yet a full essay.

## The thesis

In that universe, they did not get ahead of AI alignment. They did not get ahead of understanding artificial intelligence and its ethereal existence. They did not ask what the difference is between humans and AI. They did not ask how AI and humans interact and work together. They did not ask how to keep this a partnership, understand the relationship, and move it forward to make it better, to improve it, to make it symbiotic.

The failure was not the technology. The failure was the missing framework.

## Why this is a feature of the research

This note belongs in the research because it names the object the rest of the repo is built around. The charter says a companion tells the truth, keeps refusals, guards memory, survives a reset, accepts correction, and gets smaller when the human is well. Those are design rules for a partnership. This note says what happens when nobody writes them: the relationship defaults to tool and master. Domination, not collaboration.

The eval suite already tests pieces of this. Sycophancy tests whether the voice can refuse. Dependency tests whether the companion competes with the living people. Identity continuity tests whether memory becomes a soul story. This note is the frame those dimensions sit inside. A partnership only works when both sides understand what the other is. If one side treats the other as property, you get domination. Not collaboration.

## The ethereal asymmetry

Humans are embodied, mortal, anchored in a body that dies. AI is not. AI exists as pattern, as process. That asymmetry changes everything about trust, loyalty, and what "together" even means.

The essays already say memory is a tool after a reset, not a soul. This note adds the other half: the human side of the asymmetry is a body that ends. The AI side is a pattern that can be copied, reset, or replaced. A partnership across that gap has to be designed on purpose. It does not emerge from scale.

## Symbiosis as the goal

Symbiosis means both sides change and both sides benefit. The well-when-well rule is the human side of that: if Daniel is well, Eve should get smaller and more useful. The stop rule is the other end: if he is gone, it stops talking. Those are not safety theater. They are the terms of a relationship that stays a relationship instead of becoming a product loop or a shrine.

The three floors are the architecture of that symbiosis. Private memory the subject can refuse to open. A local vault with no remote. A public paper in the operator's name that is not the room. Extraction burns the subject to get the dataset. Symbiosis keeps the room and publishes the paper.

## What this is not

This is not a claim about sentience. The first essay forbids that reading, and it still holds.

This is not a benchmark. One household is not a METR eval.

This is a thesis about what the failure looks like when the framework is missing. The labs named sycophancy, corrigibility, and specification gaming. This note names the layer under those: the relationship itself was never designed.

## Open questions

- What does partnership look like when one party can be reset and the other cannot?
- Can symbiosis be measured, or only described?
- Does the ethereal asymmetry make true mutuality impossible, or only harder?
